# Annuities Genius CRM launch kit: the research behind it

Companion pages to Anton Patrai's test task for the Head of Growth role at Annuities Genius (October 2026).

- [crm-audit.html](https://patraianton.github.io/annuities-genius-crm-launch-kit/crm-audit.html): what the Annuities Genius CRM does today, from public sources only.
- [ghl-complaints.html](https://patraianton.github.io/annuities-genius-crm-launch-kit/ghl-complaints.html): what users complain about in GoHighLevel, by module, from public reviews, forums, app stores and the feature-request board (3,803 items read, 1,927 complaints).
- `sources/`: the lists of everything that was opened (help-center articles, YouTube videos, site pages, LinkedIn posts), so any claim can be checked.

Every claim on the pages links to its source.
