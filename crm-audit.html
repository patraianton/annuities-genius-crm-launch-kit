Emails
  • GrantAI drafts an email with To, Subject and body. Buttons: "Open in Mail" (with a dropdown arrow) and copy. No "Send" button. The report is downloaded and attached by hand. (help 114, screenshot, May 13, 2026)
  • Prebuilt prompts: "Client Communication & Sales: Appointment email, First call script, Follow-up strategy, Email to client, Email to prospect, Meeting preparation script, Proposal letter, Referral request email, Annual review invitation". (help 97, Apr 22, 2026)
  • On request, the AI adds a Calendly link and three meeting-time options to a report. (videos Mar 13 and Jul 23, 2026)
  • In the GrantAI 2.0 webinar the email opens in the agent\'s own mail app and the call happens off-platform: "after I send it, what do I do next? I guess I\'ll call the guy and let him know there\'s something cool waiting in his mailbox." (video 13:32, Mar 13, 2026)
  • AG sends the client one email itself: the link to the AI questionnaire ("send it via email directly from GrantAI"). (blog, May 6, 2026; in Dec 2025: "right now we don't have anything set up to be sent out prior", video 16:02)
  • Emails AG sends to the agent: lead alert, questionnaire alert, Case Design request, weekly team report "every Friday at 5:00 PM New York time ... from support@annuitiesgenius.com" (help 112, May 15, 2026), invoices, new sign-in.
  • Direct send from GrantAI: "one thing we added actually is you can send emails from here now... we just added that last week where it'll actually email it to the client if you're okay with it". (video 21:47, Feb 13, 2026.) Not shown on screen; the Mar 13 and Jul 23, 2026 demos show "open in mail" again.
  • Help 101: "Use AI to draft/send emails" (wording is ambiguous).

  • Mailbox sync (Gmail, Outlook), an email log on the client profile, email templates as a separate feature, bulk email, sequences.
  • Host, Oct 30, 2025: "it's not going to automate any kind of drip campaigns or anything like that" (video 25:34).
  • The blog points agents to an outside tool: "There are plenty of free CRM sites ... We like Zoho" (blog).

What this changed in the launch kit

Corrections made to the test-task kit because of this check.

  1. Texting is positioned as "now for every client, from your own number", since website-lead chat has existed since 2023 (the how-it-works block, the 2023-2025 pricing row, the Oct 27, 2025 video).
  2. Reminders are described as "the follow-up plan becomes dated reminders on the case, each drafted by GrantAI", and the to-confirm list asks whether repeating tasks still exist in the current interface.
  3. Timeline is described as extended, not new: dated activity, the E-App and uploaded transcripts are already on the case. The addition is emails, calls and texts next to them.
  4. Email: the "Open in mail" opening is kept, and the to-confirm list carries the one open question: whether direct send from GrantAI shipped after the Feb 13, 2026 statement.
  5. Calls: "calling on day one" is marked as the heaviest build in the kit, since AG has no telephony today, and the to-confirm list notes the 2020-2023 voice recorder next to call recording.
  6. Text registration: the dotted mark stays, and the to-confirm list now says that every text goes out from one shared number today, so a number per agent means a provider and a registration for each, not a one-time form.
  7. Pricing is left as a question: "every plan, or from Prime+?", since in-platform texting sat on Scale and Growth only in 2023-2025, the website and Calendly on Prime+ and Pro, and Client Management left Starter in June 2026.
  8. "Since 2019", not 2023. The press release of Oct 9, 2019 says "offers a back-end CRM and web site platform"; the May 18, 2020 pricing page says "Genius CRM — Store and manage all your contacts in one place"; help (Sep 21, 2026) says "Use the Dashboard to access your CRM".
  9. Integrations: "Wealthbox lists AG in its catalog; Redtail has a support article" (Oct 23, 2023). The Orion integrations catalog did not list AG on Oct 1, 2026.
  10. Import: "one-click import from GoHighLevel" became "one CSV import (today AG has none)". AG imports from Redtail, Wealthbox, PDF and pasted text.

Left unchanged because they match: the placement next to "Website Messages"; quotes and reports already saved to the profile; contact import from Redtail and Wealthbox; E-App prefill where the IMO has FireLight on; the 14-day trial. "Request Case Design with the conversation attached" stays, since today a request carries notes and files up to 10 MB and no conversation.
