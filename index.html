IMO desks are the agency teams that design cases for agents.

How this was made

The rules, the checker and the record of this run: github.com/patraianton/ag-growth-rules

  1. I went through AG's public record: all 93 help-center articles and 166 site pages. I searched the 103 videos and the Shorts on AG's channel by their captions. I also went through 65 pricing-page snapshots since 2019 and 54 posts by AG, its founder and its CTO: crm-audit.html
  2. I read 3,803 public records about GoHighLevel, a CRM sold to insurance agents under other brand names. I sorted 1,927 complaints by product area. The counts are close, not exact, because two research passes read the same review sites: ghl-complaints.html
  3. The annuity-agent portrait comes from 155 Reddit threads, about 60 insurance-forum threads, 769 Indeed reviews and 98 IMO sites (numbers.json). AG's voice comes from its own launches: voice.md
  4. AI agents I run did the research and wrote it, September 30 to October 2. Six simulated readers from one AI model family reviewed an earlier text in four rounds, the night of September 30. The text changed after that. The checker (step 5) is what checked this version, and no annuity agent has read it: misses.md
  5. The checker gave 189 findings on the October 1 version of this kit and none on this one: run report
  6. An agent on another AI model family (OpenAI's) tried to overturn the nine product facts. Six held, and one could not be checked from public pages. Two were overturned and narrowed to what their source shows: facts.md

8Agents keep Redtail and WealthboxToday's one-click import is one-way, so "keep them" is what the product can honestly support. Whether conversation history can go back to Redtail is answered live at 0:04. The email does not promise it.

To confirm with the team

Everything marked in the pieces above (a dotted underline on the page, square brackets in the markdown), in one place.

Product

  • The feature name Client Conversations: my proposal for the first release. Where it sits in the app is my proposal too: open any client → Case → Client Conversations.
  • Do client replies come back to the case? That needs a sync with the agent's inbox. For Gmail, it also needs Google's yearly security assessment (several weeks).
  • Direct email send from GrantAI was announced on the February 13, 2026 webinar. It is not visible in later demos or the help center. If it shipped, the email's "Open in mail" opening changes.
  • Request Case Design: the agent chooses the emails and call notes to send with the request.
  • Reminders were seen in a partner's May 2025 video. Do they still exist, and can GrantAI's follow-ups become them?
  • Team Activity shows which designed cases got a follow-up.
  • Agency-level switch: the IMO decides whether calling and texting are on for its account, and who pays.

Setup and money

  • Bringing an existing office line over (porting): how long it takes is to confirm. The email promises no date.
  • Calling: AG has no phone system today. Calling at launch is the biggest piece of work in this kit.
  • Texting from each agent's own number: today every text goes out from one shared number, (225) 535-4832.
  • Pricing, my proposal and not AG's price list: email and meetings in every plan. Calls and texts with N minutes and N texts included, then per minute and per text. Every plan, or only AG's Prime+ plan and the plans above it?
  • The webinar offer for a case built live, and the attendee offer at 0:22.

Promises AG would be making: questions for AG's compliance owner and founder

  • Data use: can AG tell agents that their clients and conversations stay out of marketing and lead generation? Can it say they are not sold and not shared with insurance companies (carriers) or other agencies?
  • Texting: how does the product record a client's yes? How do existing clients give it? What happens when a client asks to stop getting texts?
  • Recording calls: off by default for the agent, or set by the agency?
  • Texting number: is it registered in each agent's name, and does AG file it?
  • Retention: how long is each message kept, and can it be exported for audits?
  • Leaving: what goes with an agent who leaves, and who owns the book of clients?
  • The agents' launch email can go out co-signed by the IMO, or be handed to the IMO to send.
  • Support: does a person answer 949-600-7707?
